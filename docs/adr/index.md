- [0038-operator-vertical-delivery-method.md](./0038-operator-vertical-delivery-method.md) - Operator vertical delivery method: take the top-3 verticals (law → marketing → insurance, friction-ascending) to deliverable one at a time, building infra _through_ vertical-1; the unit is the **wedge** (a named client job, ~5–6 of ~12 skills); harden on fixtures+evals before infra, then a real-vendor contract-conformance check; **draft-for-review gating covers sends only** — read-side exposure, irreversible non-send actions, and reviewer fatigue are named residual risks requiring fail-closed entitlement across all action classes (per [0025](./0025-autonomy-ceilings-configurable-exposure-vs-initiation.md) + [0035](./0035-no-imposed-entitlement-defaults.md)); template is a shared core + thin delta **earned by rule-of-three** (extraction gated on vertical-2); provisional until law validates it (success metric + reversal triggers)
- [0039-operator-led-assessment-funnel.md](./0039-operator-led-assessment-funnel.md) - Operator-Led Assessment Funnel: SMD's front door — a voice-capable web-widget interview operator runs a top-caliber assessment, drafts evidence-bound findings, renders a premium report to the client portal that **withholds the read** to entice a human-owned closing call; the **load-bearing seam** is operator-captures-and-drafts / human-owns-the-verdict-and-close (the seam is also the conversion engine); caliber is made reliable by coverage-model + probe-repertoire + teach-back + talker/supervisor two-loop + **evals** (reliability is a number, not a claim); the **bottleneck is the human close, not the assessment** — cast assessments wide, ration the close; price is a **filter dial** (credit-back, capacity-set), depth a **configurable spectrum** (light = fewer domains, never lower caliber); build spearhead is the interview operator; provisional until real prospects validate it
- [0040-operator-positioning-and-why-us.md](./0040-operator-positioning-and-why-us.md) - Operator positioning & why-us (law-first GTM): the citable answer to "why you, why this" — compete with a hire (salary is the frame, not the moat); the spine is **"it runs on your firm's expertise and gets better at your firm every week"**; pillars = configurable entitlements, privacy (vendor-blind), customer control, the firm's voice, engine-agnostic + token-rich, managed-service-as-half-the-offering; "deep" = depth in the operator, not domain expertise (the client is the expert); boundary is paralegal/coordinator work, never licensed legal work; **the rip** retires send-identity-as-the-product, "we don't do legal work," fixed-limit-as-identity, and "AI employee" as the opener; all collateral (sales talk-track, marketing pages) derives from this ADR; competitive evidence in [competitive-landscape.md](../specs/operator/competitive-landscape.md) (**amends [0037](./0037-operator-thesis.md)**)

- [0046-admin-ia-service-spine.md](./0046-admin-ia-service-spine.md) - Admin IA & the service spine: the client is the hub (one record, surface follows lifecycle); `service` is a polymorphic spine (`consulting | operator`) instantiated by an accepted quote; the commercial layer (quote/agreement/invoice/payment) is universal and binds upward to the purchase, never to `service`, keeping a future `product` sibling open; two pivots (per-client hub × global-per-object); Operator is the lone divergence (commercial everywhere, runtime cockpit in one place); money bi-modal everywhere; flow-ordered nav `Home · Leads · Clients · Services · Billing · Operator · Analytics · ⚙` (**supersedes the flat six-tab nav + Entities-as-pipeline framing; IA/data-model direction only, schema + surface builds are follow-on issues**)
- [0047-operator-scheduled-jobs-mechanism.md](./0047-operator-scheduled-jobs-mechanism.md) - Operator scheduled-jobs mechanism: materialize the authored-but-inert `persona.cron[]` block into **Hermes-native cron** at bootstrap (the CLI/config registration path), mapping `wake_policy` → the `wakeAgent` no-agent gate (per [0021](./0021-leverage-hermes-native-primitives.md) Stream B); authored `customer.yaml` is the single source of truth, the agent's `cronjob` tool stays `CODE_EXECUTION` fail-closed and is **not** the schedule's origin; materialization fails closed and loud (no silent drop), verified against `hermes cron list` on the live Machine; rejects an overlay-side scheduler (reinvents the substrate, dual source of truth) (**accepted 2026-06-11; build is follow-on, customer-zero #1166 first consumer**)
- [0052-operator-portal-management-console-not-data-surface.md](./0052-operator-portal-management-console-not-data-surface.md) - The Operator client portal is a management console for the client's AI employee, not a data surface: it does exactly three jobs — **Direct** (config), **Account** (audit record of the Operator's actions), **Administer** (relationship) — mapping to the only three things SMD stores (per [security overview §2](../security/smd-services-security-overview.md)); it is **not** a system of record, a mirror of connected systems, a workspace, or vertical-specific; **no portal action touches client work** (approval happens in the native system / conversational channel — supervision is a read-only audit lens); the audit log is a **governance record (how it acted), not an activity diary (what it touched)**; client objects are referenced as **opaque connector-namespaced handles never interpreted** (`ref {connector,id}`, no per-vertical `matterRef`), so **connectability is the only thing tested per system** (corollary of [0037](./0037-operator-thesis.md)); evidence packets are the one carved exception that materializes content transiently on explicit request (**accepted 2026-06-20; kills the "Matters" data surface and the portal Approve & Send action; code follow-ons filed as issues**)
- [0053-author-built-mcp-connectors-per-customer-installed.md](./0053-author-built-mcp-connectors-per-customer-installed.md) - Author-built MCP connectors are per-customer-installed units, not baked into the overlay: a connector we must write ourselves because no vendor/community MCP exists (Smokeball is the first) is an **MCP server** (it surfaces tools), not a `build:` execute_code CLI — naming the shape ADR 0020's "BUILD" left implicit; that server is packaged as a **standalone, version-pinned unit installed onto a Machine only when its `customer.yaml` binds it** (same per-customer model as skill bodies, [0022](./0022-vertical-pack-architecture.md) Stream 2), runs **in-Machine scoped to per-customer credentials** (isolation per [0007](./0007-per-customer-machine-isolation.md), never a shared hosted service), version-pinned + drift-tracked per seat; the shared overlay stays **substrate-only** so adding a connector one client needs **never triggers an overlay release or fleet reprovision** ([0015](./0015-hermes-fork-vs-upstream.md) pin-only fork holds as connector count grows); external `mcp:` / `build:` / `synthetic:` unchanged (**accepted 2026-06-22; extends [0020](./0020-connector-strategy.md); first instance is the `pilot-smokeball` Smokeball server**)
- [0056-persona-exposure-skill-initiation-entitlements.md](./0056-persona-exposure-skill-initiation-entitlements.md) - Persona exposure + skill initiation entitlements: replaces scalar `trust_ceiling`, skill `action_ceilings`, scope entitlement fields, and mailbox entitlement overrides with sparse persona exposure plus explicit skill initiation; missing exposure fail-closes; `read` is enforcement-allowed, not customer-authored.
- [0057-operator-claude-connector-access-model.md](./0057-operator-claude-connector-access-model.md) - Operator Claude-connector access model: a firm's people reach the Operator through Claude via three owned layers — **login** = Clerk per-user OAuth with email-link sign-in to the firm mailbox (the proven path; email possession = identity = inherits firm offboarding); **authorization** = SMD's `mcp_issued_grants` table, read live per request, which is the authoritative allowance and the **instant kill switch** (explicit revoke cuts the next call regardless of the 1-day Clerk JWT TTL; no `forever` grants — bounded TTL + email-link re-auth is the mailbox-liveness renewal; no opaque tokens needed); **issuance policy** authored per-firm in `customer.yaml` (allowlist default / open JIT-grants a verified firm-domain identity, ADR 0035). The Operator is **not a per-user permission proxy**, which does **not** excuse **ethical screening** — a required fail-closed capability gated behind a written no-active-screens attestation until built. End-state is **EMA / the firm's own IdP** (when EMA supports Entra/M365); the bespoke Operator-issued sign-in ticket is demoted to optional sugar (**accepted 2026-06-27; first build is the grant table + live authorization; amends [0055](./0055-operator-is-an-employee.md)**)
- [0058-lead-generation-portfolio-and-sequencing.md](./0058-lead-generation-portfolio-and-sequencing.md) - Lead-generation portfolio & sequencing: the single objective is **find good-fit prospects and drive them to an assessment** (acquisition only); downstream capacity (incl. the assessment operator) is **explicitly out of scope** as a planning constraint; "good" = fit defined per offer, split on the AZ-local-consulting vs national-packs two-geography rule; channels sequenced by speed-to-good-prospects + readiness (warm-first for proof, measurement-first, paid gated on a validated message not capacity); living detail in [lead-gen-strategy.md](../marketing/lead-gen-strategy.md)
