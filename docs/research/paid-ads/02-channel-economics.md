# Track B: Paid-Advertising Channel Economics (2026, US)

Research date: 2026-07-05. For SMD's Managed Operator (managed AI-worker,
priced against a hire), owner-led SMBs, Phoenix-first then national, first
proof vertical = law firms.

**Sourcing caveat:** nearly all 2026 ad-benchmark figures come from ad-agency
and ad-tech blogs (WordStream/LocalIQ are the closest to primary aggregators;
the rest cite their own or aggregated data). Numbers are directional, not
audited. Treat ranges, not point estimates, as the signal.

### Google Performance Max / Demand Gen

- **No clean B2B-services CPL benchmark exists for PMax or Demand Gen** —
  a genuine data gap across every source. Sources say Search/PMax/Demand Gen
  "shouldn't be measured against a single CPL target" (sotrosinfotech, above).
- Directional: Google Search is the most efficient paid channel for B2B SQLs
  at $250-$450/SQL vs LinkedIn $350-800 and Meta $300-600 (sotrosinfotech).
- **Critical B2B caveat:** PMax was built for high-volume ecommerce. For B2B,
  Search outperforms PMax: **553% ROAS (Search) vs 436% (PMax)**
  (pixis.ai/blog/advantage-vs-performance-max-head-to-head-2026). PMax risks
  burning budget on junk placements for a low-volume, high-consideration offer.

### YouTube and TikTok (brief)

- YouTube: $10-$30 pre-roll CPM baseline (hubfluence.io/resources/youtube-cpm-rates).
  Viable as awareness/retargeting layer, not a primary lead engine at pre-launch scale.
- TikTok: B2B awareness CPM $10-$18; retargeted SMB-SaaS CPL $20-$40; B2B costs
  +12% YoY (stackmatix.com/blog/tiktok-ads-for-b2b-startups). Explicitly weaker
  for B2B/local-services direct response: "B2B and local services are mostly
  harder on TikTok than on Meta"
  (digitalapplied.com/blog/tiktok-ads-benchmarks-2026-cpc-cpm-cvr-industry).
  **Verdict: not viable as a primary channel for SMD in 2026** (owner demographic
  skews older than TikTok's core; considered B2B needs a landing-page handoff).

## Task 2 — Minimum viable test budget

Governing formula (Meta): `(Target CPA x 50) / 7 = daily minimum`. Meta's
learning phase wants ~50 conversions per week per ad set to stabilize
(usewonderful.com/blog/meta-ads-learning-phase-50-conversions-per-week-help-center,
adsgo.ai/blog/meta-ads-learning-phase-budget-how-much-to-spend).

- Worked example: target CPA $60 -> ($60 x 50) / 7 = $429/day (adsgo.ai).
- **Applied to SMD:** at a realistic B2B book-a-call CPA of $150-250, the pure
  learning-phase floor is $1,000-$1,800/day to exit learning on conversion
  optimization — unrealistic pre-launch. The workaround: optimize for a cheaper
  upstream event (landing-page view, form-start, lead) so you hit 50/week at a
  sane budget, then judge quality manually.
- Google uses a lighter heuristic: ~30 x CPA for smart bidding to learn (adsgo.ai).
- 50/week is a guideline, not a gate; low-volume accounts can still run with more
  variance (usewonderful.com). "Consistency of the conversion signal matters more
  than the raw number."
- LinkedIn: $3,000-$5,000/mo to gather meaningful data (stackmatix.com).

**Synthesis:** to validate one offer + one creative set on one channel with a
defensible read, budget on the order of **$2,500-$5,000 over 4-6 weeks per
channel**, optimizing to an upstream micro-conversion. Under ~$1,500 total is a
coin-flip, not a test. (No source gives one crisp number; this is synthesized
from the learning-phase math plus LinkedIn's stated minimum.)

## Task 3 — Channel fit for the buyer (owner-led SMB, Phoenix -> national)

Intent vs interruption:

- **Google = capture/intent:** buyer is actively searching; you are not
  interrupting (yellowfindigital.com/google-ads-or-meta-ads-the-answer-depends-on-intent).
  **SMD's problem: almost nobody searches "managed AI worker for my law firm" by
  name yet.** Intent channels only capture existing demand; Google Search mostly
  catches adjacent-intent terms ("law firm operations consultant," "legal intake
  automation") at legal-adjacent CPCs ($6-15+).
- **Meta = discovery/interruption:** introduces a service to someone who had not
  considered it (yellowfindigital). Structurally the right shape for a
  category-creation offer nobody searches for by name.
- Consensus: run both — Meta as discovery engine, Google as capture engine.

Reaching "the owner writes the check":

- **LinkedIn is the only channel with native role/seniority targeting** (owner,
  founder, managing partner), the sharpest instrument at a price premium; 72% of
  B2B marketers rate it highest for lead quality (postiv.ai). Meta/Google
  approximate via interests/lookalikes: cheaper reach, more waste.

Local geo (Phoenix Phase 1):

- All three geo-target tightly; Meta down to a single ZIP/neighborhood cluster
  (theadspend.com/resources/in-depth-analysis-geotargeting-strategies-for-local-businesses-on-facebook-google-and-linkedin-ads).
- The "76% of near-me searches visit within a day" stat is a retail/walk-in stat
  and **does not transfer** to a considered B2B purchase — flagged as a mismatch.
- **Phoenix-metro constraint is real:** Phoenix-only + law-firm-owner + LinkedIn
  is a tiny audience, pushing LinkedIn into ultra-narrow CPM ($150-300) and
  starving Meta's learning phase of volume. Geo-narrowness fights the algorithms'
  need for scale — the single biggest structural headwind for a local paid test.

Fit ranking for a category-creation B2B service to local SMB owners:
\*\*LinkedIn (precision, expensive) > Meta (discovery/interruption, category-fit)

> Google Search (captures only adjacent intent) > PMax/TikTok/YouTube (poor B2B
> fit at this scale).\*\*

## Task 4 — 2026-specific shifts

- **Meta Advantage+ / loss of manual control:** March 2026, advertisers reported
  sudden instability (fewer leads, higher costs) as Meta retired remaining manual
  controls (auditsocials.com/blog/meta-ai-privacy-policy-ad-targeting-changes-2026).
  Meta claims Advantage+ delivers +22% ROAS vs manual but with less
  visibility/control. **Direct warning:** "Advertisers in niche B2B verticals...
  report significantly worse performance, likely because Meta's models optimize
  for volume rather than specificity" (pixis.ai). SMD's niche, low-volume,
  high-value profile is exactly what Advantage+ automation handles worst.
- **Google:** Search CPC rose 12% in 2025, projected +8-10% more by Q4 2026,
  driven by AI Overviews, automation, Quality Score inflation (get-ryze.ai).
  Professional services face the highest inflation because AI-native startups
  flood these high-LTV keywords — SMD would buy into a category bidding itself up.
  Search still beats PMax for B2B (553% vs 436% ROAS, pixis.ai).
- **Privacy / signal loss:** first-party data is now "a key enabler of AI-driven
  campaign performance" (pixis.ai). SMD's CRM (~100 enriched prospects) is a
  first-party asset for custom audiences/lookalikes. Budget-shift guidance: move
  spend from audience testing to creative testing — "creative quality now has
  outsized influence on delivery" as targeting is automated away (pixis.ai).
- **Efficiency bright spot:** LinkedIn Thought Leader Ads ($2.29 CPC, ~6x more
  efficient than single-image) in an otherwise inflating market (datavinity.com).

## Decision-useful summary

1. Every channel inflated in 2026; Meta CPA +38% YoY is worst; Google
   legal-adjacent CPC +8-12%. Paid is getting more expensive as SMD would enter.
2. No channel efficiently captures SMD's category (nobody searches it by name):
   this is a discovery/interruption problem (Meta/LinkedIn), not a capture
   problem (Google Search).
3. LinkedIn is the only precise instrument for "law-firm owner in Phoenix," but
   Phoenix-local + niche-role = ultra-narrow ($150-300 CPM) that starves algos.
4. Realistic per-channel validation test: ~$2.5-5k over 4-6 weeks, optimizing to
   an upstream micro-conversion. Below ~$1.5k is noise.
5. Biggest open data gaps before spending: (a) actual Keyword Planner CPCs for
   SMD's exact terms; (b) any PMax/Demand Gen B2B-services CPL benchmark.
6. Structural counterfactual: given Phoenix-local + owner-niche +
   category-creation, paid ads may underperform Scott's existing hand-personalized
   Mode-B outbound. Paid reads as a _scale_ play, not a _first-proof_ play — it
   makes sense after a converting message exists, mirroring the cold-email Mode-A
   shelving logic in ADR 0059.
