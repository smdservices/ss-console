# Track A: Competitive Ad Intelligence — AI-Agent / AI-Employee Services for SMBs

Research date: 2026-07-05. Competitive intelligence for SMD's Managed Operator
positioning. Every claim cited; unverifiable items flagged.

**Methodology note on the Meta Ad Library:** the Ad Library
(facebook.com/ads/library) is a JavaScript SPA — direct fetch returns an empty
shell or hangs, so live ad text could not be scraped programmatically. Offers
were verified from advertisers' own landing pages (where their ads route) plus
third-party coverage. As of January 2026 the Ad Library shows an impression-range
bucket on every ad (under 1K ... 1M+), the best proxy for campaign scale once
viewed manually in a browser. A manual Chrome pass is recommended to capture
exact creatives, run dates, and impression buckets before the strategy locks.
Sources: superscale.ai/learn/how-to-use-the-meta-ad-library,
admapix.com/blog/ad-intelligence/facebook-ads-library-complete-guide

## 1. Named advertisers seen running Instagram ads (July 2026)

### smith.ai — AI + human receptionist (the cost-comparison archetype)

- Offer: 24/7 call handling by AI-assisted human agents who screen leads, run
  intake, and book qualified prospects. Legal is a named vertical.
- Pricing (public): AI Receptionist ~$95/mo (Starter, ~60 calls), $270/mo
  (Basic, ~150), $800/mo (Pro, ~450); per-call $1.60-$1.90, $2.40 overage.
  Annual "Done-for-You AI" plans price-lock at $500 / $1,000 / $2,000 per month
  with no overage + a dedicated success manager. Human tiers $292-$975/mo. Legal
  answering from ~$285/mo. Sources:
  g2.com/products/smith-ai-ai-receptionist/pricing,
  smith.ai/pricing/ai-receptionist, smith.ai/industries/legal-law-firms-answering-service
- Ad hook seen: "$48,000+/Year for a Receptionist. $6,000/Year for Smith." with a
  Without/With split-panel and "Try Free for 30 Days." The $6,000/yr = the
  $500/mo Done-for-You AI annual plan; $48k = a loaded human-receptionist salary.
  A pure salary cost-comparison creative.
- CTA / friction: "Book a free consultation" / "Try risk-free for 30 days ... ask
  for your money back." Low friction.
- Landing page (legal): leads with proof stats — "71% of legal leads prefer phone
  calls," "Only 44% of law firms actually answered their phones," "99.9%
  Accuracy." Pricing gated behind a lead form.

### Binary Studio (binary_studio) — technical custom AI-agent builds

- Offer: custom AI-agent / software development. Ad seen: "Build AI agents that
  run your ops end-to-end," deploy into ERPs & CRMs, 2-week POC, technical
  pipeline-trace visual.
- Pricing: none public; dev-shop custom-quote model.
- Verified: established custom-software / CRM-ERP dev agency (real-estate CRM,
  construction ERP+CRM, React modular CRM case studies:
  binary-studio.com/crm-implementation, binary-studio.com/case-studies/crm-system-development).
  **Could not verify the exact ad landing-page copy** — public site reads as a dev
  agency, not a productized AI-employee. Treat the ad as agency lead-gen in
  agentic language. Aimed at a more technical buyer than the "free audit" crowd.

### RapidDev (rapiddev\_ / rapidevelopers.com) — done-for-you agents, no-headcount angle

- Offer: "Build AI agents that actually do the work." Sub: "Custom AI agents that
  automate operations, eliminate repetitive tasks, and help your team move faster
  without adding headcount." Explicit "Human + AI Collaboration — agents don't
  replace your team, they make them more effective."
  (rapidevelopers.com/services/ai-agent-development-services)
- Pricing: none public — estimates after a discovery session.
- Ad hook: "Automate operations. Reduce costs. Scale faster." CTA: "Book a Free
  Consultation" / "Book an AI Agent Strategy Session." (Aggressive agency tone; a
  loud "$745k/month in 5 years" self-promo banner.)

### Youtiva (youtiva.ai / youtiva.com) — "own your AI," free audit

- Offer: "Build your own intelligence" / "Helping SMBs build their own business
  brain." Private, owned custom AI systems, delivered in under 120 days,
  money-back if ROI not shown in 90 days. Differentiator: ownership — "Shared AI
  tools level the field. Proprietary AI creates competitive advantage."
  (youtiva.com)
- Pricing: a "$19/month" Basic tier appears but reads as placeholder/template
  text; real engagements are custom builds. West Palm Beach based.
- Ad hook / CTA: "Custom AI systems built for small & medium-sized businesses" ->
  "Book your Free AI Opportunity Audit." Money-back ROI guarantee is the risk
  reversal.

### AgentsInstall.com — DONE-FOR-YOU + MANAGED, payback guarantee (SMD's closest twin)

- Offer: "AI agent teams, installed in your business." Sub: "We audit how your
  business actually runs, then build and manage AI agent teams that answer every
  lead, chase every follow-up, and clear the admin work your payroll is buried
  in." **A managed service, not a tool.** (agentsinstall.com)
- Risk reversal: "100% Payback Guarantee — if your install doesn't pay for
  itself, you don't pay."
- Salary framing: "Most first installs absorb the equivalent of one part-time to
  one full-time role: 20 to 40 hours a week." "Average owner recovery: 36 hours
  per week after 60 days."
- Process (published): 30-day install — Days 1-3 audit, 4-10 design agent roles,
  11-21 build/connect/test in shadow mode, 22-30 launch + tune. Safety:
  "Sensitive actions pause for human approval," scopes/logs/fallbacks/escalation.
- Pricing: none public — "it depends on workflow complexity," free audit first.
- Ad hook / CTA: "AI AGENTS INSTALLED FOR YOU — No code. No setup headaches." ->
  "Book Your Install Call" / "Book the free workflow audit."
- **The single closest competitor to SMD's Managed Operator model** — DFY +
  ongoing management + audit entry + payback guarantee + role-replacement framing.
  Key differences vs SMD: horizontal (no named vertical), self-serve "no code"
  language, agent-_teams_ framing rather than one named worker, and no
  human-firm/practitioner positioning.

## 3. Adjacent field — who else spends in the "AI worker for SMB" space

### A. Self-serve AI-employee products (software, not managed service)

- Lindy (lindy.ai): markets "AI employees" — role-shaped agents (SDR, scheduler,
  support rep) configured through chat; SMB self-serve (Plus ~$49.99/mo); owns the
  "AI employee" content term heavily (lindy.ai/blog/ai-employee,
  open.cx/blog/lindy-ai-review-and-alternatives-2026).
- 11x (11x.ai) and Artisan: AI SDR/BDR products; enterprise/negotiated pricing;
  sales-team buyer, not owner-led SMB ops (getbreakout.ai/blog/11x-vs-artisan-ai-sdr-platform).
- Marblism (marblism.com): "AI Employees to scale your business," bundle of 6 from
  ~$24/mo (yearly); self-serve "digital workforce" framing (marblism.com/pricing).
- Synthflow (synthflow.ai): no-code AI voice agents; PAYG, ~$29-$899/mo; product,
  not service (pxlpeak.com/blog/ai-tools/synthflow-pricing-guide).
- Air.ai: AI voice agent, $25k-$100k upfront license + per-minute — an outlier
  high-ticket, demo-gated model (lindy.ai/blog/airai-pricing).

### B. AI-automation agencies selling done-for-you to SMBs

This is the crowded lane and where the ad-buying energy is. Dominant playbook
(RapidDev, Youtiva, AgentsInstall, plus a long tail of solo "AI automation
agency" operators): free audit / free consultation -> discovery -> custom build ->
(sometimes) ongoing management, priced by custom quote, sold with a no-headcount /
cost-reduction hook and often a money-back or payback guarantee. AgentsInstall is
the most productized and the only one explicitly selling ongoing management as the
core.

Known-scaled signals: Smith.ai is the clearest long-running, well-funded
advertiser (the salary split-panel is a mature, repeatedly-run format).
Self-serve products (Lindy, Marblism, Synthflow) run continuous performance
campaigns. The agencies look like leaner lead-gen buys — not sizable without the
manual Ad Library pass.

## 4. Pattern synthesis

### (a) Offer archetypes that dominate

1. Free audit / free AI-opportunity assessment (Youtiva, AgentsInstall, RapidDev).
2. Book-a-call / free consultation (RapidDev, Binary Studio, Smith.ai).
3. Cost-comparison vs a salary (Smith.ai's "$48k vs $6k"; AgentsInstall in
   labor-hours).
4. Free trial / money-back / payback guarantee (near-universal risk reversal).
5. Done-for-you install (AgentsInstall).
6. Own-your-AI / proprietary-advantage (Youtiva; less common).

### (b) Creative formats

- Split-panel Without/With cost comparison (Smith.ai) — high-clarity for
  cost-anxious buyers.
- Product-UI / pipeline-trace proof (Binary Studio).
- Benefit-stack text over abstract AI glow (RapidDev, Youtiva, generic agencies).
- "Installed for you / no setup headaches" simplicity (AgentsInstall).
- Thin on: founder-face video, named-customer testimonials, human-team imagery.
  The field skews faceless/product-abstract.

### (c) CTAs and friction

- Overwhelmingly low-friction lead-gen: "Book a free [audit/consultation/call]."
  The ad is never the conversion event; the call or email sequence is.
- Risk reversal used to lower friction further.
- Almost nobody advertises a hard price except Smith.ai (as a comparison).

### (d) Whitespace gaps — angles nobody (or almost nobody) is running

1. **The human+AI firm — a real team behind the worker.** The field is faceless.
   No advertiser sells "a firm of experienced operators who run a managed AI
   worker for you." Smith.ai has humans but frames them as a call-center safety
   net, not a guiding firm. SMD's practitioner-firm positioning is uncontested in
   ad creative.
2. **Vertical-specific managed AI worker for law-firm operations.** Smith.ai /
   LawDroid / LegalClerk own law-firm phone intake — but nobody advertises a
   managed worker for the core operational lifecycle (matter/case coordination),
   exactly SMD's A&P wedge. The legal AI ad space is 100% front-desk, 0%
   back-office operations.
3. **Named, single, managed Operator (a "who," not a "team of agents").**
   Competitors sell "AI agents" (plural, abstract) or "AI employees" (product
   SKUs). Nobody sells one named managed operator that is your person.
   AgentsInstall is closest but says "agent teams."
4. **Priced-against-a-salary managed retainer** (not a tool seat, not a custom
   build quote). Smith.ai compares to salary but sells call-volume tiers. The
   clean "one flat monthly price, less than a hire, and we run it" positioning is
   largely open.
5. **Objectives-first / collaborative framing.** Everyone leads with problems,
   automation, cost-cutting. Nobody leads with "understand where you're trying to
   go." Differentiated but a softer hook — likely better on the LP than in the
   cold-scroll ad.

Direct answers:

- Managed "we run it for you" angle: yes, AgentsInstall (and Smith.ai in
  answering). Not crowded; room remains, especially with a firm/vertical spin.
- Vertical-specific: only in phone intake (Smith.ai, LawDroid, LegalClerk,
  CaseGen). Nobody in law-firm operations. Open.
- Human+AI firm vs pure software: essentially nobody. Whitespace.

## 5. Law-vertical specific

The legal lane is active and entirely concentrated on inbound phone intake /
answering / reception, not case operations.

- Smith.ai (legal): AI + human receptionists, 24/7 intake, Clio/Lawmatics sync,
  bilingual, from ~$285/mo; ads lead with intake proof stats
  (smith.ai/industries/legal-law-firms-answering-service).
- LawDroid: conversational AI, smart call routing, conflict checks, flat $400/mo
  unlimited calls (getnextphone.com/blog/best-ai-tools-for-law-firms-2026).
- LegalClerk AI: flat $400/mo unlimited calls, "legal-only AI" (same roundup).
- CaseGen.ai: "Answering Service AI for Law Firms" (casegen.ai).
- Clio Manage AI (formerly Clio Duo): AI inside the practice-management suite — a
  connection-target / incumbent, not an intake advertiser
  (clio.com/resources/ai-for-lawyers/ai-tools-for-lawyers).
- Gavel, LawDroid Copilot: document-automation / drafting, not advertised as
  "staff."

Ad/offer shape in legal: flat-rate unlimited-call pricing ($400/mo emerging
anchor), "never miss a call / after-hours intake" hook, Clio integration as table
stakes, free-consultation CTA. **All front-of-funnel. The back-office operational
lifecycle — matters, deadlines, discovery coordination, client status — is
advertised by no one.** That is precisely where SMD's A&P / law pack sits, and it
is uncontested in paid media.
