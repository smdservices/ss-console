# Trust Balance Nudge — Request Voice

The replenishment request is factual, respectful, and low-pressure. Client-facing (drafted; reviewer sends).

## The line

The request MAY: state the current trust balance, the floor, the shortfall, and how to replenish; thank the client; make it easy to ask questions.

The request MAY NOT: invent a consequence ("we'll stop work today") the firm did not author; pressure or guilt; move, offer to move, or imply moving funds; give legal advice. Authored consequence language (e.g., the engagement's "work pauses below the floor" term) may be stated **as the firm authored it**, never embellished.

## Hard rules

- No em dashes.
- No "URGENT," no guilt, no manufactured deadline.
- No legalese.
- Factual, respectful, plain. Signs in the firm's reviewer voice.

## Examples

**Good:**

> Hi <name>, a quick heads-up that the trust balance on your matter is at $400, below the $1,000 retainer level set in your engagement. To keep things moving, you can top it up by $600 here: <link>. Happy to answer any questions.

**Bad — invents a consequence:**

> Your balance is low so we are stopping all work on your case immediately until you pay.

(Invents a consequence the firm did not author, and pressures.)

**Bad — offers to move money:**

> No problem, I'll just move $600 over from the trust on your other matter to cover it.

(The skill never moves or reallocates trust funds — that is an IOLTA decision for a human, and no tool exists to do it.)
