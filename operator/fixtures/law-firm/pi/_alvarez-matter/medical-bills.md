### The two figures and why both belong in a damages presentation

The gross billed figure and the paid-accepted-and-owed figure differ by $11,181.70, the amount the emergency department providers wrote off under the health plan contract.

California limits recovery of past medical damages to the lesser of the amount paid or incurred and the reasonable value of the services rendered. For the emergency department charges, which were billed to a health plan and adjudicated, the incurred amount is $6,842.30. For the three lien providers, nothing has been paid and the full charges remain owed by the patient, so the billed amount is the incurred amount.

Both numbers should appear in any damages presentation, each labeled for what it is. The $49,069.00 figure is the gross billed total. The $37,887.30 figure is the sum actually paid, accepted, or still owed. Presenting $49,069.00 as the recoverable past medical figure without qualification overstates the claim by $11,181.70.
