## Flagged items

### FLAG 1: Treatment gap of 48 days, August 20, 2024 to October 7, 2024

**Nature.** The only interval in the course of care exceeding the routine threshold. No treatment of any kind occurred between the trigger point injection visit of August 20, 2024 and the epidural steroid injection of October 7, 2024.

**Documented explanation, contemporaneous.** The gap is explained in the chart at the time it began, not afterward. The August 20, 2024 note records that the epidural steroid injection was recommended and that the patient asked to defer it until after her return to full duty, for stated reasons: the procedure requires a driver and a recovery day, her husband could not take time off, her childcare covered only her work hours, and she did not want to interrupt her return to full work status. The note records that she was advised the deferral was her choice and that the injection remained available.

**Corroboration at the far end.** The October 7, 2024 note records that she contacted the office on September 30, 2024 to schedule the procedure, which is six days before the visit and one day before her scheduled return to full duty of October 1, 2024. The same note records that she managed the interval with her home exercise program, over-the-counter ibuprofen, and the TENS unit dispensed on May 31, 2024.

**Assessment.** This is a documented deferral, not a lapse in treatment or a gap in symptoms. The explanation exists at both ends of the interval and in the patient's own recorded reasons at the time. The dates align: deferral requested August 20; return to full duty directed for October 1; office contacted September 30; procedure performed October 7.

### FLAG 2: Records outstanding, Valley Imaging Center

**Nature.** The MRI report and films from the August 7, 2024 study have not been produced. The findings enter this record only through the treating orthopedist's summary in his August 20, 2024 note, and his office did not retain a copy of the written report.

**Status.** Requested through the records vendor June 20, 2026. Open on the matter task list. Weekly chase cadence.

**Effect.** The billing and the lien for the study are documented. The imaging findings themselves are presently secondhand.

### FLAG 3: Prior lumbar history, three years before the incident

**Nature.** A single prior episode of low back pain in May 2021, disclosed by the patient at the emergency department and taken in detail at the initial orthopedic consultation.

**As recorded.** Lifting injury at work; treated by primary care with medication and a home exercise program; resolved in about six weeks; no imaging; no injections; no specialist referral; no restrictions on discharge; no recurrence and no back treatment of any kind between 2021 and the incident.

**Scope.** The prior history is lumbar only. The records contain no prior cervical complaint, injury, or treatment of any kind.

### FLAG 4: No future care, permanency, or impairment documentation

**Nature.** A completeness flag, not a defect.

**As recorded.** The final evaluation of December 17, 2024 discharges the patient from active care, assigns no work restriction, directs continuation of the home exercise program and over-the-counter medication as needed for a flare, and instructs her to follow up as needed.

**What the records do not contain.** No physician opinion that further treatment will be required. No estimate or projection of future medical cost. No permanency opinion, impairment rating, or disability percentage. No permanent work restriction. No life care plan, vocational assessment, or future earning capacity analysis.

**Effect on the file.** Any statement about future care, future medical expense, permanent restriction, or impairment would not be supported by any document in this record.
