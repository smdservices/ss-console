17   A.  I saw it in the left lane, yes. I wasn't watching it.
18       There was no reason to.
19   Q.  Did the truck use a turn signal?
20   A.  No.
21   Q.  How can you be sure?
22   A.  Because I was looking right at the back of it when it
23       came over. If there was a blinker I would have seen
24       it. There wasn't one.
