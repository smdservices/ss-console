## 1. Who we represent and what this agreement covers

This agreement is between you, Maria Alvarez, referred to in this letter as "you" or "the Client," and this law firm, referred to as "the Firm," "we," or "us."

**The Claim.** We agree to represent you in connection with claims for personal injury and related damages arising out of the motor vehicle collision that occurred on May 23, 2024, on Calle Verde Road in Los Angeles County, in which your vehicle was involved in a collision with a vehicle operated by Kenneth Draper. We refer to this throughout as "the Claim."

**What is included.** Our representation includes investigation of the Claim, communication with insurance carriers, negotiation of a settlement, and, if necessary, filing and prosecuting a lawsuit through trial in the trial court.

**What is not included.** This agreement does **not** cover any of the following, and we are not obligated to perform any of them unless we sign a separate written agreement with you:

- Any appeal, whether taken by you or by another party, and any proceeding after entry of judgment.
- Enforcement or collection of any judgment.
- Any claim for damage to your vehicle or other property, unless we notify you in writing that we have agreed to handle it. We will assist you at no additional fee in presenting your property damage claim to the insurance carriers, but property damage recoveries are not subject to the fee described in section 3.
- Any workers' compensation claim, disability claim, bankruptcy matter, criminal matter, family law matter, immigration matter, or any tax consequence of a recovery.
- Any claim against any person or entity other than those responsible for the Claim.
- Negotiation or litigation with your own health insurer, medical providers, or lienholders after your matter concludes, except as described in section 6.

If a matter arises that is outside this agreement, we will tell you, and you will not owe us a fee for it unless we have agreed in writing to handle it.

## 2. Notice: our fee is not set by law

**The fee described in this agreement is not set by law. It is negotiable between you and the Firm.**

This notice is required by California Business and Professions Code section 6147. You are free to negotiate the rate, to seek other counsel, or to decline to sign this agreement.

## 3. Our fee

We are paid only if you recover. This is called a contingency fee. If there is no recovery, you owe us no attorney fee at all.

Our fee is a percentage of the **gross recovery**, meaning the total amount recovered on the Claim before any deduction for costs, liens, or any other charge. The percentage depends on the stage at which the Claim resolves:

| When the Claim resolves                 | Fee                                      |
| --------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| By settlement before a lawsuit is filed | **33 1/3 percent** of the gross recovery |
| After a lawsuit is filed                | **40 percent** of the gross recovery     |

"Recovery" includes money paid by settlement, arbitration award, or judgment, and includes any amount paid on your behalf, any structured settlement (valued at its cost to the payer), and any medical payments or uninsured or underinsured motorist benefits recovered through your own insurance policy on account of the Claim.

**This agreement does not cover any appeal.** If an appeal is taken, we will discuss it with you and, if we agree to handle it, we will sign a separate written agreement setting the fee for that work.

**No fee is charged on the property damage portion** of your claim.

**We do not guarantee any result.** We have made no promise about the outcome of the Claim, the amount of any recovery, or how long the matter will take. Any opinion we give you about the Claim is only our professional judgment and is not a promise or a guarantee.

## 4. Costs and how they affect your recovery

**Costs are different from attorney fees.** Costs are the out-of-pocket expenses of pursuing the Claim. They include, for example: court filing fees, jury fees, service of process, court reporters and deposition transcripts, videography, charges for obtaining medical records and billing records, medical illustration, investigators, photocopying and postage, messenger and delivery charges, electronic research, mediator fees, arbitrator fees, travel expenses, and expert witness fees. Expert witness fees in a personal injury case can be substantial.

**We advance costs.** We will advance the costs of pursuing the Claim. You are not asked to pay costs as the case proceeds.

**How costs are repaid, and how they affect what you receive.** Costs we advance are reimbursed to the Firm out of your recovery. **Our fee is calculated on the gross recovery, before costs are deducted.** This means costs are subtracted from your share, not from ours, and they reduce the amount you ultimately receive. The order of distribution is:

1. Our attorney fee, calculated on the gross recovery under section 3.
2. Reimbursement to the Firm of costs we advanced.
3. Payment of medical liens, provider balances, and any reimbursement claims that must be paid from the recovery.
4. The balance to you.

We will give you a written settlement statement showing every one of these amounts before any money is disbursed, and you will have the opportunity to review it and ask questions.

**An illustration.** These figures are used only to show how the arithmetic works. They are not a prediction and they are not an estimate of your case.

> Assume a case that settles after suit is filed for a gross recovery of $100,000, with $8,000 in advanced costs and $20,000 in medical liens.
> Attorney fee: 40 percent of $100,000 equals $40,000.
> Costs reimbursed to the Firm: $8,000.
> Medical liens paid: $20,000.
> Net to client: $32,000.

**If there is no recovery.** If we recover nothing, you owe us no attorney fee. You also will not be asked to reimburse the costs we advanced. The Firm absorbs them.

**One exception you should understand.** If the Claim goes to trial and you lose, a court can order you to pay certain of the opposing party's costs. This is a matter of law and is separate from our agreement. The same is true if a statutory settlement offer is made and not accepted, and you later fail to obtain a more favorable result. We will advise you at the time if this risk becomes real, and we will discuss it with you before any decision that creates it.

## 5. What we will do, and what we ask of you

**Our obligations.** We will provide competent representation, keep you reasonably informed about significant developments, promptly respond to your reasonable requests for information, explain matters to the extent reasonably necessary for you to make informed decisions, and act in your interest.

**Your decisions, not ours.** **The decision whether to accept or reject any settlement offer is yours alone.** We will convey every offer to you and give you our recommendation, but we will not settle your case without your approval. The decision whether to file suit and the decision whether to go to trial are also yours.

**What we ask of you.** So that we can do our work:

- Keep us informed of your current address, telephone number, and email address.
- Tell us promptly about any change in your medical condition, any new treatment, any new provider, and any change in your work status.
- Attend medical appointments, and tell us if you stop treating and why.
- Cooperate in discovery, which may include answering written questions under oath, producing documents, and giving a deposition.
- Be available for court appearances, the deposition, mediation, and trial.
- Tell us the truth, completely, including facts you think are unhelpful. Prior injuries, prior claims, and prior accidents are almost always discovered by the other side. We can address a difficult fact if we know about it. We cannot address one we learn about for the first time in a deposition.
- Do not discuss the Claim with anyone other than us. This includes any adjuster or investigator for any insurance company. If someone contacts you about the Claim, refer them to us.
- Be careful about social media. Assume that anything you post may be seen by the other side and used in your case.

**Attorney-client privilege.** Communications between you and the Firm about the Claim are confidential and privileged. That protection can be lost if you share those communications with other people. If you are unsure whether something is protected, ask us first.

## 6. Medical bills, liens, and health insurance

You remain responsible for your medical bills. We do not become responsible for them by representing you.

Some of your providers may agree to wait for payment until your case resolves, in exchange for a lien on your recovery. If you sign a provider lien, you are agreeing that the provider will be paid from your recovery. We will help you understand any lien before you sign it.

Your health insurer, your medical payments coverage, or a government program may assert a right to be reimbursed from your recovery for what it paid. These claims must generally be resolved before your funds can be released.

**We will attempt to negotiate reductions of liens and provider balances at no additional fee.** We cannot promise that any lienholder will agree to a reduction. Any amount that must be paid to a lienholder is paid out of your share as described in section 4.

## 7. Money we hold for you

Any funds we receive on your behalf will be deposited into the Firm's client trust account. We will notify you promptly when funds are received. Before any disbursement, you will receive a written settlement statement itemizing the gross recovery, our fee, the costs reimbursed, every lien or provider payment, and the net amount payable to you. Disbursement occurs after you have reviewed and approved that statement.

If any part of a distribution is disputed, we will hold the disputed portion in trust until the dispute is resolved.

## 8. Ending the representation

**Your right to discharge us.** **You may discharge the Firm at any time, for any reason or for no reason, by telling us in writing.** This right is absolute and it does not depend on our agreement.

If you discharge us, or if we withdraw for good cause, we may be entitled to be paid for the reasonable value of the work we performed before that point, and to be reimbursed for the costs we advanced. Any such payment is due only out of a later recovery on the Claim, and only to the extent permitted by California law. If there is never a recovery, we are paid nothing.

**Our right to withdraw.** We may withdraw from representing you as permitted or required by the California Rules of Professional Conduct. Examples include a conflict of interest, your failure to cooperate in the Claim, your insistence on a course of action we believe is unlawful or without merit, or a breakdown in the attorney-client relationship. If we withdraw after suit is filed, we must obtain the court's permission. We will give you reasonable notice and time to obtain other counsel.

**Conclusion of the representation.** Our representation ends when the Claim is resolved and the funds are disbursed, or when we are discharged or withdraw. When the representation ends, we will send you a letter confirming it. We do not undertake to advise you about the matter after that date, and we do not undertake to monitor any deadline that arises afterward.

## 9. Your file

The file is yours. At the conclusion of the representation, or at any time on your written request, we will provide you with your file at no charge, in the format we maintain it. We may keep a copy for our records.

Unless you instruct us otherwise in writing, we will retain your file for five years after the representation ends and may destroy it after that period. If you want your file, ask us for it before then.

## 10. Disputes about fees

If a dispute arises between us about fees or costs, **you have the right to require that the dispute be resolved by arbitration through a State Bar approved fee arbitration program** under California Business and Professions Code sections 6200 through 6206. That arbitration is voluntary for you and mandatory for the Firm if you request it. We will give you the required written notice of that right before commencing any action against you concerning fees.

Nothing in this section requires you to arbitrate any claim other than a dispute about fees or costs.

## 11. Professional liability insurance

The Firm maintains professional liability insurance covering the services described in this agreement, and will maintain it or comparable coverage throughout the representation.

## 12. No other agreements

This letter is our entire agreement about the subject it covers. It replaces any earlier discussion or understanding, whether spoken or written. It may be changed only by a writing signed by both of us.

If any provision of this agreement is found to be unenforceable, the rest of the agreement remains in effect.
