### Procedure

**Lumbar interlaminar epidural steroid injection at L4-L5 under fluoroscopic guidance.**

Informed consent obtained after discussion of risks including bleeding, infection, nerve injury, dural puncture, headache, and steroid side effects. Timeout performed. The patient was positioned prone. The lumbar region was prepped with chlorhexidine and draped in sterile fashion. The L4-L5 interlaminar space was identified fluoroscopically. Skin and subcutaneous tissue were anesthetized with 1 percent lidocaine. A 20-gauge Tuohy needle was advanced using loss of resistance to saline. Position was confirmed in anteroposterior and lateral projections with 2 mL of contrast demonstrating appropriate epidural spread without vascular or intrathecal uptake. A total of 80 mg methylprednisolone in 3 mL of preservative-free normal saline was injected. The needle was withdrawn. A dressing was applied.

The patient tolerated the procedure well. No immediate complication. Observed for 30 minutes. Vitals stable. Discharged with a driver.

Fluoroscopy time 42 seconds.
