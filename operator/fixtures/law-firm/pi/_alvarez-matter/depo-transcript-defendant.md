### Evasive passage

**Page 25, line 11 through page 26, line 9.** Asked what he said at the scene, the witness answers only that he asked whether she was okay, then answers "I don't recall" to whether he said anything else. Asked directly whether he said "I thought I had room," he answers "I don't recall saying that." Pressed on whether he is denying it or does not remember, he answers "I don't remember." He then does not remember what he told the officer, and does not remember the words.

The sequence matters because Witness Grace Lindqvist told the investigating officer at the scene that she heard the defendant say words to that effect, and that he said it more than once. The witness does not deny the statement. He declines to remember it.

### Note on the citation testimony

At 27:11 to 27:14 the witness testifies that he did not contest the citation and paid it. Defense counsel objected and moved to strike as to the disposition, citing Vehicle Code section 40834, which provides that a plea, a finding, or a forfeiture on an infraction is not admissible in a civil action. That objection is well founded as to the disposition. The fact that a citation was issued, and the investigating officer's conclusions, stand on different footing. Any use of this passage should be limited accordingly.
