## 3. Wage loss computation

### 3A. Off-work period, May 24, 2024 through July 8, 2024

**Interval.** 46 calendar days. 32 weekdays fall in the interval, of which 3 were company paid holidays (Memorial Day May 27, Juneteenth June 19, Independence Day July 4). **29 scheduled workdays were missed.** For overtime purposes the interval is 32 weekdays divided by 5, or **6.4 scheduled work weeks**.

| Component          | Computation                                                               | Amount        |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------- |
| Lost regular wages | 29 workdays x 8 hours x $27.40                                            | $6,356.80     |
| Lost overtime      | 6.4 weeks x 3.2 hours equals 20.48 hours, rounded to 20.5 hours, x $41.10 | $842.55       |
| **Subtotal**       |                                                                           | **$7,199.35** |

The three paid company holidays are excluded from the claim because she was paid for them.

### 3B. Modified-duty period, July 9, 2024 through September 30, 2024

**Interval.** 84 calendar days, exactly **12.0 weeks**.

## 4. Paid time off used

Ms. Alvarez drew **40 hours of accrued paid time off** during the off-work period, valued at **$1,096.00**, paid in the June 2024 pay periods. Her accrued balance was reduced accordingly and had not been fully restored as of the date of the employer verification letter.

This amount is disclosed rather than omitted. Under the collateral source rule, benefits the plaintiff received from a source independent of the defendant do not reduce the defendant's liability, and paid time off is an earned benefit of employment. It has not been deducted from the claim above.

Ms. Alvarez received **no** state disability insurance benefits, **no** short-term or long-term disability benefits, and **no** workers' compensation benefits for this absence. The collision did not arise out of or in the course of her employment. She was driving home from work at the time and had left the premises.

## 5. What is not claimed

Stated affirmatively so that the boundary of this claim is unambiguous.

- **No loss of future earning capacity is claimed.** No physician has assigned any permanent restriction, and no vocational or earning capacity analysis has been performed or obtained. Ms. Alvarez returned to her same position, at her same rate, with her same overtime, and has worked without restriction since October 22, 2024.
- **No loss of a promotion, raise, or bonus is claimed.** The employer verification letter confirms no change in rate or standing. Her position carries no bonus component.
- **No household services or replacement services claim is presented in this document.** Ms. Alvarez testified in deposition that her husband and her sister performed household tasks she could not perform during her recovery. No such services were paid for and no invoice or valuation exists.
- **No claim is made for the October 7 through October 21, 2024 restricted period**, which the employer accommodated at full hours.
