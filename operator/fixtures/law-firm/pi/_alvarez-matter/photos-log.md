# Photographic Evidence Log: Alvarez v. Draper

**Matter:** Alvarez v. Draper, LASC 24STCV18223, firm matter 2026-PI-101
**Log maintained through:** July 24, 2026

**What this log is.** An inventory of the photographic evidence in the matter file. Each entry describes what an image shows, when it was taken, who took it, how it came into the file, and where it sits in the Bates ranges. The images themselves are not reproduced here.

**Totals.** Seven sets, 77 images. Sets A through E are plaintiff-side and have been produced. Set F is the defense production. Set G is in the investigating agency's file and has been requested.
