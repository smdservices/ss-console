## Deficiency analysis

Prepared for the meet and confer letter. Grouped by the defect, with the statutory basis.

### Group 1: Requests for Production 1 through 5

| Request | Defect                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Authority                    |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| 1       | Objections only, with no statement of compliance, representation of inability to comply, or objection-based withholding statement. A response consisting solely of boilerplate objections is not a code-compliant response. The objections themselves are unsupported: "burdensome" requires a factual showing that is absent.                                                                                      | CCP 2031.210(a); 2031.240(b) |
| 2       | Privilege asserted and documents withheld with **no privilege log**. A responding party withholding on privilege grounds must provide sufficient factual information to permit an evaluation of the claim. The objection is also overbroad on its face: the request expressly excludes communications with counsel, so it cannot be that every responsive communication is privileged.                              | CCP 2031.240(c)(1)           |
| 3       | "Equally available to the propounding party" is not a recognized objection to a demand for production of documents in the responding party's possession, custody, or control.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | CCP 2031.220; 2031.230       |
| 4       | **"A diligent search was not completed" is a facial violation.** A representation of inability to comply must affirmatively state that a diligent search and reasonable inquiry **has been made**, and must specify whether the inability is because the item never existed, has been destroyed, has been lost, or is in the possession of another. This response states the opposite of what the statute requires. | CCP 2031.230                 |
| 5       | Reference to "documents previously produced" without identifying them by Bates range or by category. Documents produced must be identified with the specific request to which they respond.                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | CCP 2031.280(a)              |

## Substantive value in the compliant responses

The compliant portions of this production carry more weight than the deficient portions.

**The signal contradiction.** Response to Form Interrogatory 20.4, verified June 30, 2026 and served July 1, 2026, states that responding party "activated his turn signal and checked his mirrors before changing lanes." Response to Request for Admission No. 2 denies that he failed to signal. Both are verified.

Seven and a half weeks earlier, on May 7, 2026, the same party testified under oath that he had no specific memory of moving the signal lever, no memory of seeing the indicator, and, in his own words, "No, I can't. I couldn't tell you for sure." Deposition of Kenneth Draper, 23:15 to 23:24.

This is a verified writing contradicting the party's own sworn testimony on the central liability fact. It is impeachment material and it is a credibility argument, and it is worth more than the discovery motion.

**The admissions.** Requests for Admission Nos. 1, 3, and 10 are admitted. The defendant admits he changed lanes immediately before the collision, admits he did not see the plaintiff's vehicle before doing so, and admits his vehicle carried a hitch receiver extending beyond the bumper. The first two establish the mechanism. The third answers the low-damage argument before it is made.

**The work order.** DEF 000013 through DEF 000015 shows a job concluding at 5:15 p.m. in Montebello and a 6:00 p.m. appointment in Whittier. The collision occurred at 5:42 p.m. The document corroborates the testimony at 22:6 to 22:19 that he was running behind, and it is the defendant's own business record.

**The policy limits.** DEF 000010 through DEF 000012 establishes $100,000 per person and $300,000 per occurrence, with no umbrella, no excess, and no commercial policy. That is the ceiling on the recoverable insured amount and it frames the mediation.

**The unsearched phone records.** The single most consequential deficiency is Request No. 4. The defense theory accuses the plaintiff of mobile telephone use, and Request for Admission No. 1 in defendant's own RFA set to plaintiff asks her to admit exactly that. The plaintiff produced her call detail records, which show her last call ended at 5:31 p.m., eleven minutes before the collision. The defendant, asked for the same category of document about himself, responded that a diligent search **was not completed**. His deposition testimony at 24:17 to 24:23 places him looking at his phone at about the same time as the lane change.
