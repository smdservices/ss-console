## Physical Evidence

Tire friction marks were observed in the eastbound number 2 lane, laid down by Party 2. Two parallel marks, the left measuring 22 feet and the right measuring 20 feet 6 inches, terminating at the area of impact. The marks were photographed and measured from the north curb line.

The area of impact was established at a point 180 feet west of the prolongation of the west curb line of Sandoval Avenue, and 6 feet 4 inches south of the north edge of the eastbound number 2 lane, based on the termination of the friction marks, a gouge mark in the asphalt, and the origin of the fluid trail.

A debris field consisting of grille fragments, headlamp lens fragments, and plastic bumper material extended approximately 31 feet east of the area of impact within the number 2 lane.

A fluid trail of green coolant extended from the area of impact east to the Party 2 point of rest.

Both vehicles came to rest in the eastbound number 2 lane, Party 1 forward of Party 2, separated by approximately 8 feet. Party 1 came to rest with the left rear quarter of the vehicle approximately 2 feet across the lane line dividing the number 1 and number 2 lanes.

No pre-impact friction marks were attributable to Party 1.

## Statements

### Party 1, Kenneth Draper

Draper stated he was eastbound on Calle Verde Road in the number 1 lane, returning from a job site and traveling to another appointment. He stated he needed to make a right turn at Sandoval Avenue and moved into the number 2 lane to do so. He stated he was traveling about 35 miles per hour.

He stated that after he moved into the number 2 lane, a vehicle ahead of him in that lane slowed to turn right and he applied his brakes. He stated he was struck from behind "almost right away."

Asked whether he signaled the lane change, Draper stated, "I always signal." Asked whether he specifically recalled signaling on this occasion, he stated he could not say for certain and that he "would have."

Asked whether he saw the Honda before changing lanes, Draper stated he checked his mirror and did not see a vehicle. Asked how much space he had when he moved over, he stated, "Enough, I thought."

Draper stated he was not using a mobile telephone at the time of the collision. He stated his telephone was mounted on the dashboard.

### Party 2, Maria Alvarez

Alvarez stated she was eastbound on Calle Verde Road in the number 2 lane traveling about 40 miles per hour, on her way home from work. She stated the traffic ahead of her in her lane was clear.

She stated a white pickup "came over from the left lane right in front of me" and that she did not see a turn signal. She stated the pickup "wasn't all the way in the lane yet" when its brake lights came on. She stated she braked hard and could not stop in time. She estimated the pickup was two to three car lengths ahead of her when it entered her lane.

Alvarez stated she was not using her telephone and that her telephone was in the center cupholder. She stated she had ended a call before turning onto Calle Verde Road.

She complained of neck pain, low back pain, and a headache. She declined ambulance transport.

### Witness 1, Aaron Petrosyan

Petrosyan, age 41, of 715 Dunmore Street, Pico Rivera, was driving eastbound in the number 2 lane approximately two to three car lengths behind the Honda. He provided the following account at the scene:

"I was behind the silver car. The white truck was in the fast lane and came across into our lane, right in front of the silver car. There wasn't much room. Then his brake lights came on. The silver car locked it up and I heard the tires. I saw the whole thing. I didn't see a blinker on the truck. I would have seen it because I was looking right at him when he came over."

Asked specifically whether he was certain about the turn signal, Petrosyan stated, "I'm certain I didn't see one. I was watching him because he came over fast."

Petrosyan provided a telephone number and remained at the scene until released. He stated he was willing to be contacted.

### Witness 2, Grace Lindqvist

Lindqvist, age 63, is employed at Sandoval Avenue Auto Parts, located at the northeast corner of Calle Verde Road and Sandoval Avenue. She was on the sidewalk outside the business at the time of the collision. She provided the following account at the scene:

"I heard tires screeching and then the bang. By the time I looked up it was already over. I didn't see how it happened. The truck was sitting kind of crooked, halfway into the other lane. The lady in the car was holding her neck. The man from the truck got out and walked back and I heard him say something like, 'I thought I had room.' He said it more than once."

Lindqvist stated she did not observe the movement of either vehicle before the impact. She provided a telephone number and a work address and stated she was willing to be contacted.

## Summary

On Thursday, May 23, 2024, at approximately 1742 hours, Party 1 (Draper) was driving vehicle 1 eastbound on Calle Verde Road in the number 1 lane, west of Sandoval Avenue, at a stated speed of approximately 35 miles per hour. Party 2 (Alvarez) was driving vehicle 2 eastbound on Calle Verde Road in the number 2 lane, to the rear and right of vehicle 1, at a stated speed of approximately 40 miles per hour.

Party 1 initiated a lane change from the number 1 lane to the number 2 lane in order to position his vehicle for a right turn at Sandoval Avenue. Party 1 entered the number 2 lane in front of vehicle 2. Independent Witness 1, who was traveling behind vehicle 2 in the number 2 lane and had an unobstructed view, stated that vehicle 1 entered the number 2 lane directly in front of vehicle 2 with limited spacing and that no turn signal was displayed.

Shortly after entering the number 2 lane, Party 1 applied his brakes in response to an unidentified vehicle ahead that had slowed to turn right at Sandoval Avenue.

Party 2 applied her brakes, leaving 22 feet of tire friction marks, and the front of vehicle 2 struck the rear of vehicle 1 within the number 2 lane. Both vehicles came to rest in the number 2 lane.

Party 2 complained of pain to the neck and back and reported a headache. She was evaluated at the scene by Los Angeles County Fire Department personnel and declined ambulance transport. Party 1 reported no injury.

## Opinions and Conclusions

### Primary Collision Factor

The primary collision factor is attributed to Party 1, Kenneth Draper, for a violation of **Vehicle Code section 22107**, turning or moving right or left upon a roadway without reasonable safety and without giving an appropriate signal.

This conclusion is based on the physical evidence, the position of vehicle 1 at rest partially within the number 1 lane, the statements of both parties placing vehicle 1 in the number 1 lane immediately before the collision, and the statement of independent Witness 1, who observed the lane change and stated that no turn signal was displayed. Party 1 was unable to state that he had signaled and acknowledged that he did not see vehicle 2 before moving into the number 2 lane.

### Other Associated Factor

An other associated factor is attributed to Party 2, Maria Alvarez, for **Vehicle Code section 21703**, following more closely than is reasonable and prudent. Party 2 was unable to stop within the available distance after vehicle 1 entered her lane. This factor is noted as a contributing circumstance only and is not the primary collision factor. No citation was issued to Party 2.

### Sobriety and Distraction

Neither party displayed objective signs of alcohol or drug impairment. No field sobriety tests were administered and no chemical tests were requested.

Both parties denied mobile telephone use at the time of the collision. No mobile telephone records were requested or reviewed as part of this investigation, and no opinion is offered on that subject.

---

## Recommendations

Party 1, Kenneth Draper, was issued Notice to Appear number **LA-4471902** for a violation of Vehicle Code section 22107. The citation was issued at the scene at 1841 hours and signed by Party 1.

No citation was issued to Party 2.

No further enforcement action is recommended.
