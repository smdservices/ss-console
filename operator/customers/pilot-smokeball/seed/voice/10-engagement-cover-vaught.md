**How you pay us, in plain terms**

You do not pay us anything out of pocket, ever, at any point.

We are paid a percentage of what we recover for you: one third if the case resolves before we file a lawsuit, forty percent if we have to file. If we recover nothing, you owe us nothing and you do not owe us the costs either. That is section 3 of the agreement you signed and I want you to know it without having to find it.

Case costs are things like the police report, medical records, filing fees, expert witnesses, and depositions. We advance all of them. They come off the top at the end, out of the recovery, not out of your pocket along the way.

There is one more piece of the arithmetic that most people do not hear until the end, so you are hearing it on day one. If your health insurance pays any of these medical bills, they get reimbursed out of the settlement. That is called a lien. It is negotiable, we negotiate it hard, and every dollar we knock off it goes to you and not to us.

The gross number is never the take home number. Anyone who tells you otherwise is setting you up to be disappointed in eight months.
