This is my meet and confer letter on Trammell's responses to Form Interrogatories, Set One, Special Interrogatories, Set One, and Requests for Production, Set One. I am writing it in good faith and I would genuinely rather fix this on the phone than in a courtroom.

**Form Interrogatories 20.1 through 20.11**

Every one of these is answered with the same eleven word objection block and then a reference to the traffic collision report.

Form 20.2 asks your client to state how the incident occurred. A police report is not your client's contention. Form 20.5 asks whether Trammell contends anyone else contributed to the incident, and the answer is either yes with names or no. Form 20.10 asks for the vehicle's inspection and maintenance history, which is squarely within Trammell's possession and has nothing to do with the collision report at all.

A boilerplate objection followed by a document reference is not a substantial compliance under section 2030.220. If you have a contention, state it. If you do not, say so, and I will hold you to it at trial.

**Special Interrogatories 3, 7, 8, 12, and 14**

These ask about the brake service history on the tractor, the hours of service records for driver Ruben Castellanos in the seventy two hours before the collision, and Trammell's dispatch communications that morning.

The responses are "Objection: vague, ambiguous, overbroad, burdensome, and not reasonably calculated to lead to the discovery of admissible evidence."

Two points on that. First, "reasonably calculated to lead to the discovery of admissible evidence" left the Federal Rules in 2015 and has never been the California standard for what is discoverable under section 2017.010. Second, none of these is vague. Special 8 asks for the date of the last brake service on tractor unit 4471 before June 2, 2025. That is a date.

**Requests for Production 4, 5, 9, 11, 15, and 16**

You produced 212 pages. Every one of them is the collision report, the photographs I gave you, and my client's own medical records.

You produced no maintenance file. No driver qualification file. No hours of service logs. No electronic logging device data. No dispatch records. No post collision drug and alcohol test results, which under 49 C.F.R. part 382 Trammell was required to obtain and retain.

Your response to Request 15 says responsive documents "will be produced." That was thirty two days ago.

Silence is not a response and a promise is not a production.

**The preservation issue, which is the one I am actually worried about**

Electronic logging device data on most fleet telematics platforms rolls off on a six month retention cycle unless it is affirmatively held. The collision was June 2, 2025. We are past thirteen months.

I sent Trammell a litigation hold letter on July 18, 2025, four days after I was retained, addressed to their registered agent and copied to their risk department. If that ELD data is gone, I need to know now, and I need to know when it went and who was told what.

Please confirm in writing, by July 20, that the telematics data for unit 4471 for June 1 through June 3, 2025 has been preserved. If it has not, tell me that instead. I would rather have a hard answer than a soft one.
