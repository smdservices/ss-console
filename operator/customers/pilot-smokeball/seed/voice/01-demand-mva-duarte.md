**What happened**

August 14, 2025, 7:42 a.m. Ms. Duarte was northbound on Orizaba Avenue in Long Beach, on her way to work. She entered the intersection at Willow on a green light.

Kyle Brennan was westbound on Willow in a Ford Transit van owned by Copperline Logistics, Inc., loaded and on route. He was in the eleventh minute of his shift and running behind by his own account.

He did not slow down. He struck Ms. Duarte's 2018 Honda Civic at the driver's door and pushed it into the northeast curb.

Officer R. Sandoval of the Long Beach Police Department investigated and prepared Traffic Collision Report 25-0088412. He cited Mr. Brennan under Vehicle Code section 21453(a). Mr. Brennan told him he "thought it was still yellow."

Adrienne Kowalczyk was stopped at the limit line on Willow, two cars behind the van, waiting for her own light to change. She has no connection to either party. She told Officer Sandoval that the van never braked and that her light had been red long enough that she had already picked up her phone. Her recorded statement is enclosed at Tab 4.

Mr. Brennan was driving a company vehicle, on a company route, during company hours. Copperline is liable for what he did.

**The injuries**

Two things broke that morning.

The first is her left wrist. The end of the forearm bone at the wrist snapped and shifted out of position (displaced distal radius fracture). It required surgery. On August 21, Dr. Vivian Ostrander at Long Beach Orthopaedic Group opened the wrist, reset the bone, and fixed it with a plate and seven screws. The hardware is still in.

The second is her neck. The cushion between the fifth and sixth vertebrae pushed out and pressed on the nerve root running down her right arm (C5-6 herniation with right C6 radiculopathy). MRI on September 30 at Pacific Imaging confirmed it. She has numbness in her right thumb and index finger and she drops things.

She tried to avoid surgery. Thirty four visits of physical therapy at Coastline PT. Two epidural steroid injections at Harbor Pain and Spine, October 22 and December 4. The relief from the second injection lasted nine days.

On January 20, 2026, Dr. Warren Achebe at Pacific Spine Institute recommended a single level fusion at C5-6. His written estimate is $96,400. It is enclosed at Tab 9. She has not scheduled it yet, because she is the only earner in her household and she is trying to work out how to be off her feet for ten weeks.
