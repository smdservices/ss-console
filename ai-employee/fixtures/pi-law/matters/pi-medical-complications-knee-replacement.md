## Liability summary

Liability theory: surgical retention of foreign object during right TKR. Plaintiff's expert (Dr. Mathis, board-certified orthopedic surgeon) opines that retention of the fragment was a deviation from the standard of care for total knee replacement and was the proximate cause of the revision surgery and downstream complications. Defense expert (Dr. Ahn) concedes the fragment was retained but disputes that it required revision surgery, arguing the fragment was clinically insignificant and that the revision was elective.

Causation is the contested element, not breach. Defense expert's position is the principal trial risk.

This matter is not a broad medical malpractice case. It is a narrow retained-foreign-body claim with strong documentary support. The firm has explicitly declined to plead any broader negligence theories.
