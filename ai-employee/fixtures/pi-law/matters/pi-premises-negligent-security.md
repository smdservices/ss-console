## Liability summary

Liability theory: negligent security against the property owner and property management company. Discovery has surfaced the following material facts:

- Avenida Plaza had 14 documented assault or robbery incidents in the parking lot in the 36 months preceding plaintiff's attack
- Property management discontinued nighttime security patrols in 2024-06 (5 months before incident) as a cost reduction
- Three lighting fixtures in the east parking lot had been reported as inoperative for over 60 days at the time of the incident; work orders had been opened but not closed
- A 2023 security risk assessment commissioned by the property owner recommended continued nighttime patrols and lighting upgrades; the assessment was produced in discovery 2025-12

The combination of foreseeable harm (documented prior crime pattern), withdrawn protective measures (discontinued patrols), and unaddressed environmental factors (broken lighting against expert recommendation) is the spine of the liability theory.

Defendant's primary defenses: criminal-act-of-third-party intervening cause, and that the assault occurred in a tenant's portion of the lot rather than common area (disputed; survey establishes location squarely in common area).
