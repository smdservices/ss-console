## Liability summary

Liability theory: failure to maintain safe walking surface in the HOA common area. Discovery surfaced the following:

- The HOA's snow-removal contract (Mountain Snow Services) covers roadways only. Walkways are explicitly excluded.
- The HOA's CC&Rs assign walkway maintenance to the HOA itself, not to individual unit owners.
- The HOA has no written or de facto procedure for snow/ice treatment of walkways.
- Three prior incident reports from the same subdivision in the trailing 24 months involved slips on similar untreated walkway surfaces.

Defense theory: open-and-obvious doctrine, arguing the icy walkway was visible and plaintiff should have been aware. Defense also pleaded comparative fault: plaintiff was wearing rubber-soled shoes rather than dedicated winter boots. Discovery has weakened both theories: walkway lighting in the area is poor (HOA records confirm three nearby fixtures were inoperative on the date of incident) and the rubber-soled shoes were the same model the plaintiff had used through prior winters.

Crestwood-style joint defendant claims against the snow removal contractor were attempted but did not survive summary judgment (contractor's scope of work did not include walkways).
