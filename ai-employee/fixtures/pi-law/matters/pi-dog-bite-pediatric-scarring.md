## Liability summary

Liability is reasonably clear under Arizona's strict liability dog-bite statute (A.R.S. § 11-1025). Defendant dog owner is strictly liable for damages caused by the bite. Comparative fault is not a recognized defense to a strict-liability dog-bite claim in Arizona except in narrow circumstances not present here (child was on common property, not provoking the dog).

Open liability question for partner review: whether to plead negligence against the Riverview HOA in addition to strict liability against the dog owner. The HOA had two prior complaints about this owner's leash compliance and took no enforcement action. HOA liability would unlock the HOA's commercial general liability policy, which is likely materially larger than the homeowner's policy carried by the dog owner. Partner has flagged this for review against AZ HOA-liability case law.
